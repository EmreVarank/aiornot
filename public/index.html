Frequently Asked Questions

When using the Gemini API, accuracy is significantly higher as a real LLM analyzes the text. Heuristic-only mode uses 6 statistical signals and is less reliable on short or ambiguous texts. Always treat results as estimates, not verdicts.

You can paste plain text directly, or upload PDF and DOCX files up to 10MB. Text is extracted automatically from documents before analysis.

No data is ever stored by this app. If you use the Gemini API, your text is sent directly to Google's API servers per their privacy policy. Heuristic analysis runs 100% locally in your browser.

Burstiness measures variation in writing rhythm. Humans naturally alternate between short punchy sentences and long complex ones. AI models tend to maintain a more consistent, predictable sentence length throughout a passage.
