Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

AI Checker operates with a strict zero-retention policy. When you use our service:

  • Text you submit: Analyzed in memory and immediately discarded after results are generated
  • IP address: Used only for rate limiting and security purposes
  • Usage analytics: Anonymous, non-identifying statistics about service performance

2. How We Use Your Data

Your submitted text is used solely to provide AI detection analysis. We do not:

  • Store your text in any database
  • Use your text to train models
  • Share your text with third parties
  • Use your text for any purpose beyond the immediate analysis

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google's Gemini API to enhance our detection accuracy. Text is transmitted to Google's servers for analysis. Google processes this data according to their privacy policy. We recommend reviewing Google's privacy practices for details on how they handle submitted content.

4. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all connections
  • Content Security Policy headers
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • Regular security audits

5. Cookies & LocalStorage

We use browser storage only for:

  • Theme preference: Your dark/light mode choice (stored in localStorage)

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics that identify you personally.

6. Children's Privacy

AI Checker is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we become aware a child has provided information, we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Since we don't retain your data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. Your analysis results exist only in your browser session.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. We will notify users of material changes via email or prominent notice on our website.

9. Contact Us

For privacy concerns or questions, contact us at privacy@aichecker.app
